Common questions

I'm not good with computers. Can I still do this?

Yes. If you can send a text message, you can do this. And with the $500 plan, you don't have to do anything at all. We do it all.

What is Canva? Do I have to pay for it?

Canva is a free website for editing pictures. The 6 posts open there. A free Canva account is all you need. You click on the words and type your own.

Is it safe to pay online?

Yes. Payments go through Stripe, a payment company used by millions of businesses. We never see your card number. Your card statement may show the name of our parent company, Silarius.

Can I stop a monthly plan?

Yes, anytime. No contract. It just stops at the end of the month you paid for.

Do I own what you make?

Yes. The posts and the website are yours to keep, even if you stop working with us.

What's a "domain"? Is it included with the website?

A domain is your web address, like yourcompany.com. It's not included, because you should own it yourself. It costs about $10–20 a year, and we'll help you set it up.
