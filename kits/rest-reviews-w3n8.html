Google Review Reply Kit for Restaurants & Cafes (25 replies)

Find the reply that matches the review, change the words in (brackets), and paste it into Google. Answering every review, good or bad, shows new customers you care.

Tip: save this page to your bookmarks so you can come back to it anytime. Tap "Copy" next to any item to copy it.

5-star reviews

  1. Thank you, (name)! We're so glad you loved the (dish). See you next time!
  2. (Name), this made our whole kitchen smile. Thanks for coming in and for the kind words.
  3. Thank you for the five stars, (name)! Next time, try the (other dish). We think you'll love it too.
  4. So happy you enjoyed your (occasion) with us, (name). Thanks for choosing (restaurant)!
  5. Thank you, (name)! (Server name) will be thrilled to hear this. We'll pass it along.
  6. Thanks, (name)! Reviews like yours help a local spot like ours more than you know.

4-star reviews

  1. Thanks for the great review, (name)! We'd love to earn that fifth star next time. What could we do better?
  2. Thank you, (name)! Glad you enjoyed the (dish). We're always working to make every visit a 5.
  3. We appreciate it, (name)! Thanks for the feedback on (detail). We're on it.

3-star reviews

  1. Thanks for the honest review, (name). We'd like to hear more about what didn't hit the mark. Please call us at (phone).
  2. Thank you, (name). Sorry the (issue) wasn't up to our usual standard. We'd love another chance to get it right.
  3. We appreciate the feedback, (name). We've shared it with our team. Please come back and see the difference.

1 and 2-star reviews

  1. (Name), we're sorry. This is not the experience we want for anyone. Please call (manager name) at (phone) so we can make it right.
  2. We hear you, (name), and we apologize. We've talked with our team about (issue). We'd like to invite you back on us.
  3. Thank you for telling us, (name). We take this seriously. Please email (email) so we can look into exactly what happened.
  4. Sorry we let you down, (name). That's not who we are. We'd really like a chance to fix this. Please reach out at (phone).

Common complaints

  1. Slow service: We're sorry about the wait, (name). We were short-staffed that night, and that's on us. We've added help on busy nights.
  2. Cold food: Sorry your food arrived cold, (name). That should never happen. We've reviewed our kitchen timing with the team.
  3. Wrong order: We apologize for the mix-up, (name). Please call us and we'll make your next order right, on us.
  4. Prices: Thanks for the feedback, (name). We use (quality detail), which costs more, but we hear you. Check out our (deal) for great value.
  5. Delivery: Sorry about your delivery, (name). We'd like to make it up to you. Please call us at (phone).

Reviews with no words

  1. Thank you for the five stars, (name)! We hope to see you again soon.
  2. Thanks for the rating, (name)! We'd love to hear what you ordered next time.
  3. Thank you, (name)! Tell us how we can earn five stars next time.
  4. Thanks for stopping by, (name)! We appreciate you.
