30 Specials & Promo Captions for Restaurants & Cafes

Copy a caption, change the words in (brackets), add a photo of the food, and post. Use them for daily specials, slow nights and holidays.

Tip: save this page to your bookmarks so you can come back to it anytime. Tap "Copy" next to any item to copy it.

Daily specials

  1. Today's special: (dish) for (price). Only until it's gone.
  2. Taco Tuesday is here. (Deal) all day, dine in or takeout.
  3. Wednesday means (special). Your midweek treat is waiting.
  4. Thursday special just dropped: (dish). Tag who you're bringing.
  5. Friday feels better with (dish). Open until (time) tonight.
  6. Weekend brunch: (brunch items). Served (time) to (time) Saturday and Sunday.
  7. Chef's pick of the week: (dish). Ask your server about it.

Slow-night boosters

  1. Rainy day? We'll bring the food to you. Order delivery at (link).
  2. Kids eat (deal) every (day). Family night, sorted.
  3. Happy hour (time) to (time): (drink deal) and (food deal).
  4. Show this post on (day) and get (offer). Tonight only.
  5. Date night for two: (meal deal) for (price).
  6. Industry night! Restaurant workers get (discount) every (day).

New items & behind the scenes

  1. New on the menu: (dish). Made with (ingredient). Come try it first.
  2. This is what 6 AM looks like in our kitchen. Fresh (item) every morning.
  3. Meet (name), the hands behind our (dish). (Number) years and counting.
  4. Our (dish) recipe hasn't changed since (year). Some things you don't mess with.
  5. Back by popular demand: (dish). Here for a limited time.
  6. Taste test day! Help us pick our next menu item. Vote in the comments: (option A) or (option B)?

Holidays & events

  1. Celebrate (holiday) with us. Reservations open now at (phone).
  2. Game day special: (deal) during every (team) game.
  3. Mother's Day brunch is booking fast. Reserve your table at (phone).
  4. Catering for (event)? We feed groups of (number) to (number). Call (phone).
  5. Gift cards make the best last-minute gift. Buy them at the counter.

Reviews & community

  1. "(Review quote)". Thank you, (name)! Comments like this keep us going.
  2. Proudly serving (city) since (year). Thank you for every visit.
  3. Loved your meal? A quick Google review helps us more than you know. Link in bio.
  4. Big thanks to (local partner) for the fresh (ingredient). Local tastes better.
  5. Tag a friend who needs (dish) in their life.
  6. We're hiring! Join our team as a (role). Apply in person or DM us.
