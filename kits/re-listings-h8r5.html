30 Listing & Market Post Captions for Real Estate Agents

Copy a caption, change the words in (brackets), add photos of the listing, and post. Works on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn.

Tip: save this page to your bookmarks so you can come back to it anytime. Tap "Copy" next to any item to copy it.

Just listed

  1. JUST LISTED in (neighborhood): (beds) bed, (baths) bath, (feature). Offered at (price). DM me for a private showing.
  2. New on the market! This (home type) in (city) has (top feature) and (second feature). Open house (day) at (time).
  3. If you've been waiting for (feature) in (neighborhood), this is it. Swipe through, then call me at (phone).
  4. Pool, (feature) and (feature) under (price) in (city). Just listed and it won't last.
  5. Walk through (address/area) with me. Full video tour in my bio.

Open house

  1. OPEN HOUSE this (day), (time) to (time) at (address). Come see it, and bring your questions.
  2. Can't make the open house? I'll give you a private tour. Text me at (phone).
  3. Thanks to everyone who came to (address) today. (Number) groups through the door!

Under contract & sold

  1. UNDER CONTRACT in (number) days! Congrats to my sellers in (neighborhood).
  2. SOLD! Welcome home, (buyer first names). It was an honor to help you find this one.
  3. Sold over asking in (city). Thinking about selling? Find out what your home is worth. DM me "VALUE".
  4. Another happy family in (neighborhood). Keys handed over today.

Market updates

  1. (City) market update for (month): median price (price), homes selling in about (days) days. What it means for you, in plain English.
  2. Is now a good time to buy in (city)? Here's what I'm seeing this month.
  3. Rates are (up/down) this week. Here's how that changes your monthly payment on a (price) home.
  4. Top 3 things buyers in (city) want right now: (thing), (thing), (thing).
  5. New listings in (city) this week: (number). Price drops: (number). Want the full list? Comment "LIST".
  6. Florida buyers: don't forget to budget for insurance. Here's what I'm seeing on (home type) in (city).

Tips for buyers & sellers

  1. First-time buyer? Here are 5 steps from pre-approval to keys. Save this post.
  2. 3 cheap fixes that help a home sell faster: (fix), (fix), (fix).
  3. What does a buyer's agent cost you? Here's the honest answer.
  4. Hurricane season checklist for homeowners: shutters, insurance documents, photos of your home. Save this.
  5. Selling this spring? Start these 3 things now: (thing), (thing), (thing).
  6. Renting vs. buying in (city): on a (price) home, the monthly payment is about (amount). Rent for the same home is about (amount).

About you

  1. Hi, I'm (name), your local (city) agent. (Number) years helping families buy and sell here.
  2. Why I love (neighborhood): (reason 1), (reason 2), (reason 3).
  3. Client love: "(review quote)." Thank you, (client first name)!
  4. My favorite part of this job? Moments like this one.
  5. A day in my life as a (city) agent: (morning), (afternoon), (evening). Never boring.
  6. Know someone thinking about moving? I'm always happy to help. Referrals are the biggest compliment.
