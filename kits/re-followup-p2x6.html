20 Lead Follow-Up Scripts for Real Estate Agents

Use the texts for fast replies and the emails for longer follow-ups. Change the words in (brackets) and send from your own phone or email.

Tip: save this page to your bookmarks so you can come back to it anytime. Tap "Copy" next to any item to copy it.

New online lead: first 5 minutes

  1. Hi (name), this is (agent) with (brokerage). I saw you looked at (address). Want to see it this week? I have (day) or (day) open.
  2. Hi (name)! Thanks for your interest in (address). Quick question: are you already working with an agent?
  3. Hi (name), (agent) here. I can send you a list of homes like (address) that aren't online yet. Want it?

No reply yet

  1. Hi (name), just checking you got my message about (address). Still interested?
  2. Hi (name)! Homes like (address) are moving fast in (city). Want me to send similar ones as they come up?
  3. Hi (name), I'll stop texting after this one. If you ever want help with your search, I'm here: (phone).

After a showing

  1. Thanks for coming out today, (name)! What did you think of (address)? Honest thoughts help me find the right one.
  2. Hi (name), I found 2 more homes like the one you liked. Want to see them (day)?
  3. Hi (name)! The sellers at (address) just got another offer. If you're interested, let's talk today.

Sellers

  1. Hi (name), thanks for asking about your home's value. I'll have a price range for you by (day). Any recent updates I should know about?
  2. Hi (name)! A home like yours just sold on (street) for (price). Want to know what that means for yours?
  3. Hi (name), still thinking about selling? Here's what's selling in (neighborhood) this month: (link)

Long-term follow-up

  1. Hi (name)! Just checking in. Are you still planning to move in (season/year)?
  2. Hi (name), happy (holiday)! Thinking of you. Let me know if I can help with anything real estate.
  3. Hi (name), rates moved this week. If you want, I can have a lender run new numbers for you. No pressure.

Emails

  1. Subject: Homes like (address). Hi (name), here are 3 homes in (city) with (feature) that match what you're looking for: (links). Want to see any of them this week? (Agent), (phone)
  2. Subject: Your (neighborhood) market update. Hi (name), homes in (neighborhood) sold for an average of (price) last month, in about (days) days. Want a free value estimate for your home? Just reply to this email. (Agent)
  3. Subject: Still searching? Hi (name), it's been a few weeks since we talked. If your plans changed, no problem. If you're still looking, reply with your must-haves and I'll send you new homes as they come up. (Agent), (phone)
  4. Subject: Thank you! Hi (name), congratulations again on your new home! If you know anyone thinking about buying or selling, I'd be grateful for the introduction. (Agent)
  5. Subject: Your home's 1-year anniversary. Hi (name), one year ago today you got the keys! Here's what homes like yours are worth now: (value range). Let me know if you ever need anything. (Agent)
