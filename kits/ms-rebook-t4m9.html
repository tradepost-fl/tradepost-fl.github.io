20 Rebooking Text Scripts for Med Spas & Salons

Copy a text, change the words in (brackets), and send it from your business phone. Each one is short so it reads like you typed it.

Tip: save this page to your bookmarks so you can come back to it anytime. Tap "Copy" next to any item to copy it.

Right after the appointment

  1. Hi (name)! Thanks for coming in today. Your (service) looked amazing. Want me to save your next spot for (date range)?
  2. Hi (name), (provider) here. How are you feeling after your (treatment)? Any questions, just text me back.
  3. Thanks again, (name)! Quick favor: if you loved today, a Google review means the world to us: (review link)

Time to come back

  1. Hi (name)! It's been about (weeks) weeks since your last (service), which is usually the perfect time to book again. Want (day) or (day)?
  2. Hey (name), your (treatment) is due for a touch-up. I have (time) on (day) open. Should I hold it for you?
  3. Hi (name)! Just a reminder that results from (treatment) last longest with regular visits. Ready to book your next one?

Openings & cancellations

  1. Hi (name)! A spot just opened (day) at (time). You're the first person I thought of. Want it?
  2. Last-minute opening today at (time) for (service). Reply YES and it's yours.
  3. Hi (name), we added extra hours on (day) this week. Want one of the new spots?

Clients who haven't been back

  1. Hi (name)! We miss you at (business). Here's (offer) on your next visit, good until (date).
  2. Hey (name), it's been a while! Everything OK? We'd love to see you again. Want me to find you a time?
  3. Hi (name), we have new (treatment/service) since your last visit. Want to come try it? I'll save you a spot.

Reminders & no-shows

  1. Hi (name)! Reminder: (service) tomorrow at (time) with (provider). Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule.
  2. Hi (name), we missed you today. No worries, life happens. Want to pick a new time?
  3. Hi (name), just confirming you're still good for (day) at (time). See you then!

Referrals & reviews

  1. Hi (name)! Know someone who'd love (business)? Send them our way and you both get (reward).
  2. Hi (name), you've been with us (number) visits now. Thank you! Here's (gift) on your next one.
  3. Hi (name)! Would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It really helps: (review link)
  4. Hi (name), happy birthday from all of us at (business)! Here's (birthday offer) this month.
  5. Hi (name)! Our (month) specials just came out. Want me to send you the list?
